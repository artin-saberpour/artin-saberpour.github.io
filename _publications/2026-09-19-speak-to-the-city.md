This paper presents a multimodal framework for resolving user references to outside points of interest from inside a moving vehicle. The approach combines synchronized urban-transit video, vehicle telemetry, a 3D digital twin with continuous ray-casting, and a lightweight Transformer that fuses spatial gaze cues with spoken language.

It received the **Best Paper** award at AutomotiveUI 2026.
