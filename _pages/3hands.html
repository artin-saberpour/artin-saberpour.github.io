3HANDS

Learning naturalistic handovers for wearable supernumerary robotic limbs from human demonstrations.

Research

3HANDS captures human–human handovers as demonstrations of a person interacting with a hip-mounted supernumerary robotic limb. The work models handover motion, endpoint selection, and initiation timing, and evaluates naturalness and comfort in interactive use.

My contributions

I led dataset and capture design, motion preprocessing, transfer-state annotations, model architecture and training for trajectory generation, integration into an interactive VR system, and evaluation.
