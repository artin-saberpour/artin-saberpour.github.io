WRLKit

Computational design and fabrication of personalized wearable robotic limbs.

Artin Saberpour Abadian and collaborators

Read paperOriginal project

Research

WRLKit explores how wearable robotic augmentation can be personalized through computational design, fitting and fabrication. The emphasis is on customization of embodied hardware rather than autonomous learned control.
