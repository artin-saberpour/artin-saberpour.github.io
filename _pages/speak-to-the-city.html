Speak to the City

Resolving references to places and objects outside a moving vehicle through multimodal reasoning.

Research collaboration with Mercedes-Benz Tech Innovation

Original project

Research

The project studies how automotive systems can combine language and perceptual context to determine which external place or object a passenger is referring to under dynamic motion.

My contributions

I originated and led the AI methodology and multimodal fusion approach and supervised the thesis implementation.
