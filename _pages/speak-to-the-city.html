Speak to the City: Multimodal Resolution for Outside-the-Vehicle References

Alireza Parchami, Artin Saberpour Abadian, Robin Connor Schramm, Jürgen Steimle, Ulrich Schwanecke

This work addresses outside-the-vehicle referencing in dynamic urban environments. The system combines passenger head-gaze behavior with spoken language to resolve which building or point of interest the user is referring to while the vehicle is in motion.

The pipeline synchronizes 360-degree transit video with vehicle telemetry, reconstructs the environment as a 3D digital twin, performs continuous ray-casting to identify candidate buildings, and fuses spatial and semantic features with a lightweight Transformer.

Highlights

  • VR-based data collection pipeline with synchronized 360-degree transit video and GNSS telemetry
  • 3D digital twin reconstruction and continuous ray-casting for candidate building extraction
  • Multimodal Transformer combining spatial gaze evidence and natural-language intent
  • 83.33% Top-1 accuracy and 87.72% Top-2 accuracy
  • 24.3 ms average inference time
