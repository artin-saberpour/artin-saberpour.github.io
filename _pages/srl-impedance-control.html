Supernumerary Robotic Limb Impedance Control

Impedance-based control of a six-degree-of-freedom supernumerary robotic limb in simulation.

Python · MuJoCo · Impedance control · Joint-effort analysis

Source repository

System

A six-joint SRL simulation with an impedance-based controller, interactive inspection in MuJoCo and synchronized logging of six joint-effort signals.

Technical focus

Impedance-based motion control, simulated interaction, controller implementation and time-aligned visualization of generalized joint efforts.
