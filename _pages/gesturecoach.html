Research publication · UIST 2025

GestureCoach

An interactive rehearsal system that uses language-model-based recommendations to support expressive presentation gestures.

Ashwin Ram, Varsha Suresh, Artin Saberpour Abadian, Vera Demberg, Jürgen Steimle

Read paperOriginal project

Research

GestureCoach integrates language-model-based gesture suggestions into an interactive rehearsal workflow, connecting generated recommendations with user-facing rehearsal and feedback.

My contributions

I implemented and integrated the interactive rehearsal pipeline and contributed to the retrieval-augmented generation and model design discussions.
