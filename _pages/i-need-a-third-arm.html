I Need a Third Arm!

Eliciting body-based interactions with a wearable robotic arm.

Marie Muehlhaus, Marion Koelle, Artin Saberpour Abadian, Jürgen Steimle · ACM CHI 2023

Original project & video Paper PDF Publisher

Research question

How do people naturally want to communicate with a wearable robotic arm? This work investigates interaction techniques grounded in the body, including movements, gestures, gaze, and other user-proposed commands.

Method

In an elicitation study with 14 participants, people proposed interactions for robot control, navigation, object manipulation, and emergency situations, including scenarios where their hands were free or occupied. A lightweight wearable arm prototype enabled exploration without prescribing a particular sensing or control method.

Findings

The study describes user preferences for body parts and input modalities, and highlights interaction opportunities that distinguish body-worn robotic arms from off-body robots. The findings inform the design of wearable robot interfaces intended to act as tools and collaborators.
